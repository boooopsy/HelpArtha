# Custom Enclosure with Brass Inserts (P11_3)

**Module:** Industrial CAD, Parametric Modeling & 3D Printing (`module11_cad_3dprinting`)  
**Classification:** Gamma (Capstone)  

## 1. Project Objective
Design, print, and assemble a two-piece enclosure for your M10 PCB with brass M3 inserts.

## 2. Mental Model & Physical Analogy
Physical circuits need robust enclosures to manage thermal dissipation, protect against mechanical stress, and provide user-facing controls.

## 3. Bill of Materials / Tools
Digital calipers, FDM 3D printer (Bambu/Prusa/MakerBot), PLA filament, M3 brass heat-set inserts, soldering iron insertion tip.
