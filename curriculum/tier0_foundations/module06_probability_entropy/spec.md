# Module 06: Discrete Probability, Information Theory & Entropy

**Tier:** `tier0_foundations`  
**Status:** Verified Master Specification  

## 1. Concrete Mental Model
>Information is the measure of surprise. If an event is guaranteed, its information content is zero. Probability measures expectation; entropy quantifies average system-wide uncertainty.

## 2. Core Primitives
Sample spaces, mutually exclusive events, conditional probability, Bayes' Theorem; random variables, PMFs, Bernoulli and Binomial distributions, expected value, variance; information surprisal, Shannon entropy, prefix-free codes, Kraft's inequality, Huffman trees.

## 3. Physical & Virtual Workbenches (BOM)
Galton board, balanced dice, physical coin set.
