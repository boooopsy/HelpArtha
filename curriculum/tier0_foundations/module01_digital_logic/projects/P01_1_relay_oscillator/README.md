# Relay Oscillator & Buzzer (P01_1)

**Module:** Switching Mechanics, Digital Logic & Clocks (`module01_digital_logic`)  
**Classification:** Alpha (Tactile)  

## 1. Project Objective
Wire a physical relay feedback loop with a capacitor and flyback diode to create an audible oscillator.

## 2. Mental Model & Physical Analogy
Current flows or is blocked. A NOT gate is an electromagnet pulling an iron contact away from an output line. A clock is an oscillator driving the heartbeat of silicon.

## 3. Bill of Materials / Tools
Breadboard, 5V supply, Songle SRD-05VDC relays, 1N4148 diodes, 74HC08, 74HC32, 74HC04, 74HC86, 74HC74, NE555P timer, LEDs, resistors.
